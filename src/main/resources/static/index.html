Our Heritage

Dedicated to the Time-Honored Art of Slow Baking

Crumb was born out of a simple passion: to honor the rhythm of slow, honest baking. We never use artificial improvers, commercial stabilizers, or shortcuts. Every sourdough loaf is shaped by hand and cold-proved for 36 hours.

Whether it's your daily morning baguette or a 5-tier wedding cake that turns heads, we pour our heart, craft, and artistry into every bake.
